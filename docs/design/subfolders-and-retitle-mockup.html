Subfolders, Edit name and Analyse Again

Wave 2 mockup for review. Static; light above dark. Crops of the search panel's preview column (517 pt in the real 880 pt panel), Settings › General, toasts and the menu, at 1:1. Design note: docs/design/subfolders-and-retitle.md. CI snapshots of the real SwiftUI views stay the acceptance check.

The one bold moment is the Analyse Again result: the old title drops into the status line, struck through, the way setup's welcome card shows a rename. Everything else is the existing panel and native Settings.

Preview: two quiet actions on the title

Edit name (✎, ⌘E) and Analyse Again (✦, ⇧⌘E) sit at the end of the title, no fill until hover (shown on ✦). The four buttons, the Copy Text badge and the footer stay as approved. The Folder line shows the subfolder.
